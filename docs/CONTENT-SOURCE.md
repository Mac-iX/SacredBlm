## Editorial rule

Raechel's handwritten descriptions are the source of truth for voice and practice descriptions. Editing is allowed for grammar, readability, hierarchy, repetition, safety, and web comprehension. Do not replace her language with generic wellness marketing.

Avoid invented credentials, invented spiritual claims, fake testimonials, fake dates, unsupported medical claims, or polished language that changes the meaning of the practice.
